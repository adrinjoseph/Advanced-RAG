# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Advanced-RAG to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project. Everyone participating in project discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, or community spaces is expected to help maintain a welcoming environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Accept good-faith feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect different levels of experience and backgrounds.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project's goals.
- Protect private information and do not publish another person's personal data without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately misrepresenting another contributor's position.
- Unwanted sexual attention or other conduct that makes participation unsafe.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code or documentation intended to harm users.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and applying these standards fairly. They may edit or remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository profile or project hosting settings. Include relevant links or context where possible. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.
