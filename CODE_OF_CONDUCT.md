# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Advanced-RAG a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

We pledge to treat everyone with respect and to contribute to an environment
where people can learn, collaborate, ask questions, and share ideas safely.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming questions, feedback, and different perspectives.
- Giving and accepting constructive criticism professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably make participation hostile
  or unsafe.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, code reviews, and other community interactions.
It also applies when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately
through the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact
the project maintainer through the repository's available communication
channels.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.
The project maintainer will determine an appropriate response based on the
circumstances.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Possible responses include:

1. A warning and request to stop the behavior.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation in project spaces.
3. Removal of content or contributions that violate this Code.
4. A temporary or permanent ban from project spaces for serious or repeated
   violations.
